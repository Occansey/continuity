Continuity · Agentic Cinema · Gemini + ClickHouse

The department that watches the world of the story Not whether the shots match — whether the facts match, across scenes filmed weeks apart.

Everyone else compares images, so they only catch mistakes between shots that look alike. This compares claims about the world — extracted from every shot and every line of dialogue — and finds the ones that cannot both be true.

Detour (1945) is public domain. Findings are the system's own output, adjudicated by Gemini and ranked by ClickHouse; the reachability figure runs the prior art's own registration method (ORB + RANSAC) and counts where it cannot follow. A synthetic or hand-picked result would be declared here — this is not one.
